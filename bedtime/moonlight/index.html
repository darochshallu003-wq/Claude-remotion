Bramble
and the Little Lost Star
In a meadow of sleepy flowers, there lived a small hedgehog named Bramble.
One night, a tiny star tumbled out of the sky… and landed — plink! — in the clover.
“I’m lost,” whispered the little star. “And I can’t find my way home.”
So Bramble carried her up the tallest hill, step by step, past the snoring frogs.
At the very top, Old Owl spread her soft wings and flew the star back to her place in the sky.
“Thank you, Bramble,” she twinkled. “I’ll shine for you every night.”
And Bramble curled up in the clover, and fell fast asleep. Goodnight, Bramble. Goodnight, little star.
The End
