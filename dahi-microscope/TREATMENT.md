# Dahi, under the microscope — treatment

10.0 s · 1920×1080 · 24 fps · silent (no voice, no music, no foley) · English on screen

## Brief (filled from the request; no questions asked)

- **Specimen**: one real micrograph: "Yogurt Bacteria.jpg" (Andreacise, Wikimedia Commons,
  CC BY 4.0), a blue-stained yogurt smear through a 100× objective, rods and beads clearly
  resolved, photographed through the eyepiece (its circular field stop is the opening image).
  Chosen over a Gram-stained yogurt photo whose cells were an unresolved mass. Nothing on the
  specimen is drawn, retouched or generated (see `CREDITS`, `FACTS.md`).
- **Facts on screen (three, all true, plain words)**
  1. Dahi is made by bacteria: rods and chains of beads (lactic acid bacteria).
  2. They eat milk sugar and turn it into lactic acid.
  3. The acid makes milk protein clump into a soft gel: that is the set.
- **Colour world (4)**: warm glass white `#F4F1EC` · graphite bench `#0B0D0F → #2A3036` ·
  the stain's own teal-blue (evidence, never re-graded) · one accent, amber
  `rgb(255,186,84)` on stain / `rgb(150,92,0)` on glass, chosen away from the stain's hue.
- **Showpiece**: *Counting the colour*: a circle drawn as an arc on the field, a scan
  front crossing it, every stained pixel lighting up in amber behind the
  front while the share rolls up to its landing value, raw pixel counts underneath.
- **Silent film**: the sound rows below are "none" by request; motion carries the rhythm
  (a 120 bpm grid is kept anyway so beats land on a pulse).

## Three candidate structures

1. **Single dive, one measurement.** Rack in on the whole smear, log-spiral dive to a
   chain, ring it, count the purple. Clean, but ends inside the field: no "so what".
2. **Two circles, one verdict.** Measure a dense patch and a sparse one, compare. Strong
   grammar, but needs a second claim we can't support with only three facts in 10 s.
3. **Dive → count → climb.** Open already sharp on a composed mid-field (the thumbnail),
   dive to a rod and a chain (fact 1), count the purple while fact 2 runs (the showpiece),
   climb back out with the scale bar jumping back while fact 3 lands and the title is
   written on the glass.

**Chosen: 3.** It holds the style's three native moves in 10 s, every beat has a moving
camera, and the climb gives the film an ending rather than a stop. The opening is *not*
a slide on a bench (the cliché, and our source has no slide photo): it opens on stained
field, already sharp, so frame 0 is a finished image.

## Logline and arc

One continuous camera through one real smear shows that dahi is bacteria at work.
Setup (the field and its two shapes) → turn (count how much of it is bacteria, while
saying what they do) → ending (climb out: what the acid does to milk; the title).

## Benchmark — learn / don't learn

- Learn: *Powers of Ten* — one camera, a readout riding along, scale as the story.
  Pathology viewers — a quiet instrument corner (scale bar, field of view).
- Don't learn: their layouts, type, music, the earth-to-atom framing; no HUD crosshairs,
  no glow, no slide-deck headers.
