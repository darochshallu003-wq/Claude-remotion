# Cartoon series — three storylines

> **Picked: C, Fridge Gang**, with Hinglish voices (the vegetables speak short lines; the gags still carry it). Episode 1 is in `episodes/ep01/`.

Each is a whole series format; Episode 1 is the pilot. Vertical 1080×1920, 60–75 s per episode,
all characters drawn in code (SVG + GSAP), free Hindi neural voices (as in the explainers) and
synthesized music/SFX.

## A. "Mitthu Bolta Hai" — the parrot who repeats myths  (recommended)

- **Device / form:** a character does the work · one home stage (a balcony) that rebuilds each episode.
- Mitthu, a loud green parrot, repeats whatever WhatsApp forward he heard ("Raat ko dahi khaoge toh
  sardi ho jaayegi!"). Chhipkali Didi, a calm gecko with spectacles, tests the myth with a cartoon
  experiment, and Mitthu has to eat his words. It fits @mythmitao by name: *myth mitao*.
- **First 2 s:** Mitthu crashes into frame and squawks the myth, with a big shouted word on screen.
- **Beats:** myth squawk → panic gag → Didi's "Ruko!" → experiment (3 verbs) → twist fact → Mitthu
  embarrassed → "Agli baar kya sunoge?" teaser and end card.
- **Look:** sunny terracotta balcony, bold 6 px ink outlines that wobble 12×/s, flat saturated fills.
- **Sound:** bouncy ukulele-and-tabla loop, squawk and pop SFX, two voices.

## B. "Rasoi Ke Raaz" — Dadi's kitchen lab

- **Device / form:** a moment in someone's day · an endless zoom into the food.
- Chhotu asks Dadi a "why" at dinner time; Dadi shrinks them both into the food (into the dahi,
  the haldi) and the episode is a trip inside it. It carries on the channel's kitchen-science topics.
- **First 2 s:** Chhotu's spoon freezes mid-air: "Dadi, yeh kyun…?"
- **Beats:** question → shrink → three stops inside → back to full size → Chhotu tells a friend.
- **Look:** warm night kitchen, then saturated micro-worlds; soft gouache texture.
- **Sound:** pressure-cooker whistle stinger, sitar-lite music, two voices.

## C. "Fridge Gang" — silent slapstick (the bold one)

- **Device / form:** a gag short · one frame that rebuilds (the inside of a fridge).
- Vegetables in a fridge (Bhindi, Aloo, an anxious Tamatar) get into one escalating gag per episode
  when the door opens. No dialogue: music and SFX only, so it works in every language.
- **First 2 s:** the fridge light snaps on and every vegetable freezes mid-pose.
- **Beats:** door opens → plan → mishap → escalation → twist → door shuts → stinger.
- **Look:** cool blue-white fridge light, rubbery shapes, big expressive eyes.
- **Sound:** cartoon orchestra stabs, boings, door thunk; no voice.
