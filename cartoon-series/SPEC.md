# Fridge Gang — series spec

GOAL:    a viewer laughs at the freeze gag, follows the plan without sound, and wants Episode 2.
OUTPUT:  HyperFrames, 30 fps, two builds per script: 1920 x 1080 (YouTube Kids episode) and
         1080 x 1920 (Shorts). Ep 1 is a 77 s pilot; later episodes aim for 3–5 min in wide.
TIME:    the voice is the clock: each line starts 0.25–0.5 s after the last ends, never before the
         previous bubble has been up 0.8 × (0.5 s + 0.3 s a word).
LOOK:    inside of a fridge. Back wall #E2EFF7 with ribs #D2E4EF; glass shelves #BFE3F2 with white
         #F7FBFD front edges; ink #2A1B14 at 6–8 px on everything; flat fills, one shadow crescent per
         character, no gradients. Door shut = #1D3366 at 45% over the set (cool night); door open =
         full light with a 3-step flicker. Every outline boils (turbulence re-seeded 12×/s, 6 px).
         Bubbles: white, 7 px ink border, 48 px radius, Nunito 900 at 60 px; one CAPS word in the
         speaker's colour. Titles: Archivo Black 128 px, white with 12 px ink stroke and a hard 12 px
         drop. End card: mint fridge door #8FD3C1, magnet letters in the five cast colours.
OBJECTS: eggs in a tray, a steel katori of dahi, a water bottle, the crisper with dhaniya, lemon and
         Mirchi; Mummy's arm with red and gold bangles; a pizza box with a pepperoni sticker.
MOTION:  mood changes go through a take (squash 0.08 s, elastic settle 0.5 s); hops anticipate,
         stretch and land with squash; arms on back.out(2); camera cuts are hard, punch-ins 0.35–0.55 s
         power3.inOut; nobody moves in unison (offsets 0.05–0.12 s).
SHOTS:   ep01 "Bijli Gayi!" — s1 "Aloo kachaloo" sing-song, power cut, six eyes blink in the dark, title ·
         s2 the emergency torch lands on Aloo's face: "BHOOT!" · s3 Aloo's scary story, the Garam Masala
         Monster as a shadow on the wall · s4 eyes in the crisper drawer (only the audience sees them),
         then right next to Tamatar: SCREAM, hide, faint, torch flies · s5 it's Mirchi; the sneeze ·
         s6 friends, power's back · s7 the door opens, Mirchi doesn't know the freeze rule · s8 end card.
         Power cut look: set and bodies at brightness 0.2 / saturate 0.55, faces untouched (eyes glow);
         the torch spot colour-dodges the set back to its own colours.
BANNED:  dark void, glow and particles, gradients, cream paper + serif, everything centred and
         fading up, titles that drift after landing, logo docked in a corner, a sign repeating
         what the action already shows.
QA:      frame 0 shows the frozen gang in full light; every bubble readable at 360 px; the
         lid gag reads as a lid; no character cropped by the frame in wide shots.
